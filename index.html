How Shit Works

Everyday appliances, taken apart in 3D. Each one is a short story: where the power comes in, what moves, and why it does the job.

No. 01

Ceiling Fan

An outer-rotor induction motor, a capacitor to get it started, and three blades pitched at 11°.
No. 02

Toaster

Nichrome wires, a bimetal strip, and an electromagnet that holds the bread down.
No. 03

Pressure Cooker

Trapped steam at 1 bar boils water at 120°C. A 100 g weight on the vent keeps it there.
No. 04

Door Knob

A 45° turn, a square spindle, and a cam pull a spring bolt 13 mm back into the door.
No. 05

Door Lock

Five pins, one shear line. The right key lifts every pin to the same height.
